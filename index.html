A FEW IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Understanding
the audit.

What do I need to run an audit?+

A dataset with a defined outcome and audit groups, a predictor panel, and the models or prediction outputs required by the workflow. Explanation analyses also need attributions or model access. The linked TCGA study includes a concrete data-preparation and training workflow.

What does XFAIR-MED produce?+

Layer scores, subgroup diagnostic tables, support flags, and an aggregate score where the required components are available. The current TCGA workflow reports partial EXL and XCFS because it uses ASS and ERS without SAS and CSD.

Is this a clinical decision tool?+

XFAIR-MED is a research audit toolkit. Its scores describe selected properties under a defined audit protocol; they do not certify fairness, establish causality, or validate a model for clinical use.

How does the explanation layer relate to SHAP?+

Attribution methods describe which inputs influence a prediction. The explanation layer asks further questions about patterns across groups and stability under changes. Attribution consistency and explanation fidelity are different properties and should be evaluated separately.
